## Decided
- The Studio sends no usage data anywhere, opt-in or opt-out: QualiZeal works on client code, and nothing about that work leaves a laptop without a person's yes.

## Ruled out
- The anonymous, opt-out telemetry Trail-code ships (`events.nanonets.com`), when its decision trail was adopted: the method was taken, the telemetry was not.

## Watch out
- A dependency that reports home counts as sending usage data too; read a package's telemetry notes before adding it.
