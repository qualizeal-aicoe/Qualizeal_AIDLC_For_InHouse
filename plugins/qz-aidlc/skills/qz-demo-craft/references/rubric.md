# Judge rubric

Score each criterion 1-5 from the screenshots (and the deck images) only. Quote what you see as evidence.
**Pass**: average ≥ 4.0 and no criterion below 3. Anything less is "needs work" with the three fixes that would move
the score most, most important first.

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 2 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | The first screen states the prospect's problem and shows it solved; sections follow in order | A collection of widgets or slides with no arc |
| 2 | Domain realism | Their nouns, specific numbers with units, owners, dates, a believable mix of states | Generic labels, round numbers, everything green |
| 3 | Evidence and traceability | Citations, source tags, "how calculated", lineage, freshness | Claims with nothing behind them |
| 4 | Honesty | An abstain, a lag, a block, a caveat, labelled targets, "illustrative" footer | Perfect results, unlabelled projections |
| 5 | Visual craft | Design-system type scale, aligned grid, filled space, consistent colour meaning, no clipping | Uneven spacing, tiny text, empty areas, clipped text |
| 6 | Interaction (web) or density (deck) | Interactions change what is shown and matter to the story / every slide fills its area with specific content | Decorative clicks / sparse slides with generic bullets |
| 7 | Brand and voice | Client name top-left, "Demonstration by QualiZeal", plain human sentences, acronyms expanded | Wrong or invented logos, boilerplate phrasing |
| 8 | Readiness | Opens without errors, works on a laptop and a phone, nothing says sample or TODO | Errors, placeholders, broken layout on a phone |

Report as:
```
VERDICT: PASS | NEEDS WORK   (average x.x)
1 Story 4 — "Every answer, traced to its source." hero with ask box and five questions …
…
FIXES: 1) … 2) … 3) …
```
