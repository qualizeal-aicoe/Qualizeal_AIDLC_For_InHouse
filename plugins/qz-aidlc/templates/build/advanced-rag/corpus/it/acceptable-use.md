# Acceptable use of company systems

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This policy covers company laptops, phones, networks and accounts.

## Personal use

Limited personal use is allowed when it does not get in the way of work, cost the company money or break the law.

## Software

Install software only from the company app store. Software that is not in the store needs a request to IT, which
checks the licence and security before approving it.

## Monitoring

Company systems are monitored for security. Monitoring follows local law and the privacy notice; it does not include
reading personal messages unless an investigation requires it and legal approves.

## What is never allowed

Sharing your account, getting around security controls, downloading pirated content, and storing company data on
personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
