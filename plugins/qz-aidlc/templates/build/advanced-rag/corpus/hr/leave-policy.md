# Leave policy

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This policy applies to all permanent employees.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 22 working days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Part-time employees get the same
leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave builds up monthly from the first day of employment.

### Requesting leave

Request leave in PeopleHub. For three or more consecutive days, request at least 10 working days before the first
day off. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as approved.

### Carry-over

You can carry up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March
and expire after that date. Unused days above the limit are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave each calendar year. Tell your manager before your usual start time on the
first day. A medical certificate is needed after 3 consecutive days of sick leave.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver gets 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 4 weeks of paid parental
leave. Parental leave covers birth, adoption and surrogacy, and must be taken within 12 months of the child joining
the family. Give at least 8 weeks' notice where you can.

## Bereavement leave

Employees get 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for extended
family. Talk to your manager if you need more time.

## Public holidays

Each office publishes its public holiday calendar every December for the following year. Working on a public
holiday earns one day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.
