# Remote and hybrid work

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This policy covers where employees work.

## Hybrid working

Office-based roles work from the office at least 3 days a week. Teams agree their office days and publish them on the
team page. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, when everyone is reachable.

## Fully remote roles

A fully remote arrangement needs approval from a director and from the People team. It is reviewed every 12 months.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to 20 working days in a calendar year, with approval from your manager and
the People team at least 15 working days before you travel. Longer stays need a tax and immigration check first.

## Home office allowance

New remote and hybrid employees can claim a one-time home office allowance of USD 400 for a desk, a chair or a
monitor. An internet allowance of USD 40 a month is paid through payroll.

## Security when working remotely

Always connect through the company VPN. Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Lock your screen when you step
away, and never let family or friends use your work laptop.
