# Leave policy: EU addendum

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This addendum applies only to employees on a contract with one of the
company's offices in the European Union. Where it says something different from the leave policy, it wins for them.

## Annual leave in the EU

Full-time employees in the EU get 25 working days of paid annual leave each calendar year, because several EU
countries set a higher statutory minimum. Part-time employees get the same leave in proportion to their hours.

## Carry-over in the EU

Employees in the EU can carry up to 10 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 30 June.
