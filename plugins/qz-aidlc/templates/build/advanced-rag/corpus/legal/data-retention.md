# Data retention schedule

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This schedule says how long records are kept.

## Customer records

Shipment records and customs documents are kept for 7 years after the shipment. Customer contact details are kept
while the account is active and for 2 years after it closes.

## Employee and candidate records

Employee files are kept for 6 years after the employee leaves. CVs of candidates who were not hired are deleted after
12 months unless the candidate agrees to a longer period.

## Email and recordings

Business email is kept for 3 years. CCTV footage from offices and warehouses is kept for 30 days.

## Deletion requests

A request to delete personal data is answered within 30 days. A legal hold overrides this schedule: records under a
hold are kept until legal releases the hold.
