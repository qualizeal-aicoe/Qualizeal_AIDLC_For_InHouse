# New joiner onboarding

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This guide covers your first month.

## Before your first day

Your laptop arrives at least 3 working days before your start date. Your accounts are created the day before you
start, and the sign-in details come to your personal email.

## Your first week

You meet your manager on day one and agree goals for your first 90 days. You are given an onboarding buddy from
another team for your first 3 months. Questions in your first week go to the onboarding coordinator, Priya, on
+1 512 555 0147.

## Mandatory training

Complete three courses within 30 days of joining: security awareness, the code of conduct and data privacy. Your
manager is told if a course is overdue.

## Probation

Probation lasts 6 months. Your manager holds a check-in at 3 months and a review at the end of probation.
