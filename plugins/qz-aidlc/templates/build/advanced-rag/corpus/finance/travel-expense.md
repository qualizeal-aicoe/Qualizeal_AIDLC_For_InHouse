# Travel and expense policy

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This policy covers business travel and expense claims.

## Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through TripDesk, the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed
only when TripDesk could not offer the trip.

### Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more, with your vice president's
approval. Business class is not reimbursed.

### Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at USD 180 a night for domestic trips and USD 250 a night for international trips, before
taxes. Higher rates need finance approval before you book.

## Daily allowance

The per diem for meals and local transport is USD 60 a day for domestic trips and USD 90 a day for international
trips. Days of travel count as full days. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Using your own car

Business mileage in your own car is reimbursed at USD 0.58 a mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is
not business mileage.

## Expense claims

Submit claims in the expenses tool within 30 days of the end of the trip. Attach a receipt for every expense over
USD 25. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.

### Approval limits

Your manager approves claims up to USD 2,000. Claims above USD 2,000 also need approval from the finance director.
No claim is approved automatically, whatever its amount.
