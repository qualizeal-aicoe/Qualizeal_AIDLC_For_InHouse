# Salary bands 2026

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. Restricted: People team and managers only. All figures are invented.

## How bands work

Each role sits in one level from L1 to L6. A band gives the yearly base salary range for a level. New hires start
in the lower half of the band unless the People team agrees otherwise.

## Band table

- L1 Associate: USD 42,000 to USD 55,000
- L2 Professional: USD 55,000 to USD 72,000
- L3 Senior professional: USD 72,000 to USD 95,000
- L4 Lead: USD 95,000 to USD 120,000
- L5 Principal: USD 120,000 to USD 150,000
- L6 Director: USD 150,000 to USD 190,000

## Yearly review

Bands are reviewed every January against market data. Pay rises are decided in March and paid from April.
