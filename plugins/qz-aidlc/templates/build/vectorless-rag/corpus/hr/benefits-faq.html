Benefits FAQ

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. These answers cover the benefits most employees ask about.

Health insurance

The medical plan covers the employee, a spouse or partner, and up to two children. Enrol within 30 days of your joining date; after that you can only change cover at the yearly open enrolment in November.

Wellness allowance

Every employee can claim a wellness allowance of USD 300 a year for a gym, sports club, yoga classes or a fitness app. Claims go through the expenses tool with a receipt.

Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of USD 1,200 a year for courses, certifications and conferences. Your manager approves the spend before you book. Unused budget does not carry over.

Employee referral bonus

When someone you referred is hired, you receive a referral bonus of USD 1,500 once they complete 90 days with the company. Referrals for director roles and above are not eligible.

Employee assistance programme

The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling for you and your family, open 24 hours a day. You can book up to 6 sessions a year without telling your manager.
