# Leave policy (2025 edition)

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. This edition was replaced on 1 April 2026.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 20 working days of paid annual leave each calendar year.

### Carry-over

You can carry up to 3 unused days of annual leave into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave each calendar year.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver gets 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 2 weeks of paid parental
leave.
