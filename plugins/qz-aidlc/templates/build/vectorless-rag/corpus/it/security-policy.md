# Information security policy

Northwind Freight is a fictional company. Everyone with a company account follows this policy.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords are at least 14 characters long and are kept in the company password manager. Never reuse a work password
anywhere else. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every company system.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after 5 minutes without use. USB storage devices are
blocked. Store work files only in NorthDrive, the approved company cloud storage.

## Reporting a security incident

Report a suspected incident, a lost device or a phishing email within 1 hour of noticing it, through SafeReport.
For phishing, also use the Report Phish button in your mail client. Do not try to investigate on your own.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's access to systems every quarter. Access is removed on an employee's last working day.

## Using AI tools

Use only the AI assistants that IT has approved. Never paste customer data, personal data or confidential documents
into a public AI tool.
