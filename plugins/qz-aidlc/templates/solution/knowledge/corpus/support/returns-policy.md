# Returns and refunds policy

Owner: Customer support. Applies to every order placed with __NAME__.

## Return window

Customers may return an item within 30 days of delivery. A damaged or faulty item may also be returned within 30 days
of delivery, and we pay the return postage. Opened electronics may be returned within 14 days of delivery.

## Refund method

A refund goes back to the original payment method. The customer sees it within 5 business days of the refund being
issued. Store credit is offered only when the customer asks for it.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, personalised items and perishable goods cannot be returned, unless they arrived damaged.

## Starting a return

The customer starts a return from the order page, or support starts it for them with the order number. Every return
gets a return label by email within one business day.
