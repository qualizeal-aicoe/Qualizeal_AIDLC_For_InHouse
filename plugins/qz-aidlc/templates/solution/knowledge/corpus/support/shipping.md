# Shipping and delivery

Owner: Logistics.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery arrives the next business day when the order is placed
before 2 pm. Orders ship with DHL or UPS.

## Lost parcels

A parcel with no tracking update for 7 business days is treated as lost. Support opens an investigation with the
carrier and sends a replacement or a refund, whichever the customer prefers.
