# Warranty

Owner: Customer support.

## Warranty length

Electronics carry a two year warranty from the delivery date. Accessories carry a one year warranty.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage or normal
wear.

## Making a warranty claim

A warranty claim needs the order number and a photo or short video of the fault. Support replies within two business
days with a repair, a replacement or a refund.
