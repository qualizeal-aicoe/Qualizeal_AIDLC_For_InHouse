# Refund approval limits

Owner: Support operations. Internal: for the support team only.

## Approval limits

A support agent may approve a refund of up to 50.00 on their own. A refund above 50.00 needs a team lead. A refund
above 500.00 also needs finance.

## Refunds that always need a person

A refund on an order with a fraud flag, a second refund on the same order, and any refund the customer disputes always
go to a team lead, whatever the amount.
